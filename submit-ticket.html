Ticket Logging Instructions

To help us resolve your issue efficiently, please record the following typical information in the workbook. Follow the actual workbook's structure when submitting:

  • Client name or reference: Who is reporting the issue?
  • Date and time: When did the problem first occur?
  • Affected device/system: E.g., Laptop, Printer, Network drive.
  • Description of the issue: A clear summary of the problem.
  • Error messages: Exact text of any errors or relevant evidence.
  • Impact and urgency: How is this affecting your work?
  • Troubleshooting steps: What have you already attempted?
